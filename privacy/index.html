Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026

In short. Mangio works without an account. Your foods, meals, water, weight, body details and goals are stored on your phone and are never sent anywhere by the app. The app sends only a few things, listed below: a barcode when you look one up, anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (with your consent where the law asks), and what Google needs to show ads and process purchases.

Who we are

This policy covers the Mangio app for Android (“the app”) and this website. They are made by the developer of Mangio (“we”, “us”). For any question about your privacy, write to aberranttool@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in the app is stored in the app, on your phone: your foods and recipes, your diary and meals, water, weight, height, age, sex, goals, reminders and settings. Every number the app shows, from a day's calories to your weight trend, is worked out on the phone.

We have no server that holds a copy of this data, and we cannot see it. It is never included in usage statistics, crash reports, ad requests or any other request the app makes.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a packaged food that is not already on your phone, the app asks Open Food Facts for it. It sends the barcode and nothing about you or what you eat. Like any website you visit, Open Food Facts sees the connection itself, such as your IP address. Its own privacy policy applies to that request.

Usage statistics and crash reports

The app can send anonymous usage statistics, such as that a screen was opened or that the first food was logged, and reports when it crashes. They say that something happened, never what: no food, amount, meal, water, weight or goal is ever in them. They carry a random ID made by the app when it is installed, not your name or your phone's. Firebase also collects some information automatically, such as your phone model, Android version, app version, approximate country and an app instance ID.

Where the law asks for consent, nothing is collected until you agree, and you can change your answer at any time with Privacy choices in the app's More screen. Crash reports are kept by Google for 90 days, usage statistics for no longer than 14 months.

Ads

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. What you log is never given to the ad provider: no food, meal, water, weight or goal, and it is never used to choose an ad. Like any app with ads, the ad provider receives information about your phone and the ads it shows, such as your IP address, your phone's advertising ID and how you interact with an ad. Where the law asks, you choose whether ads can be personalised, with Privacy choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Premium removes the ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Premium purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play, like any app purchase. Google Play processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app receives only whether you have Premium.

Health Connect

If you connect Mangio to Health Connect, and only then, the app:

  • reads your weight, to show your weight trend in Stats and to work out your daily goal;
  • reads your height, to fill in your height in the app when it is empty;
  • writes the weights you enter in Mangio, so your other apps can read them.

Data from Health Connect is used only for these features, stays on your phone, and is never sent to us, to analytics, to crash reports, to ads or to anyone else. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used to judge creditworthiness. Mangio's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can turn this off in the app, under More, Me, Health Connect, or in Health Connect itself.

Backups

You can save all of your data to a backup file from the app. You choose where the file goes, and it is only shared with the place you choose, such as a folder on your phone or a cloud drive of your own.

Android can also back up your apps' data to your Google account, so a new phone can get it back. If that is on in your phone's settings, it includes Mangio's data. That backup belongs to your phone and your Google account: the app never sees it or sends anything for it. You can turn it on or off in your phone's settings, under Backup.

No account, no personal details

The app never asks for your name, email address or phone number, and every feature works without signing up. If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use them only to answer you.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies: usage statistics, crash reports and personalised ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Privacy choices. Non-personalised ads, barcode lookups and purchases are based on providing the app you asked for and keeping the free app possible (legitimate interest and contract). Health Connect data is used only with the permission you give in Health Connect.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete data about you, object to or restrict its use, and complain to your data protection authority. The data you log is on your phone and under your control: you can change it in the app, and deleting the app or its data deletes it. Usage statistics and crash reports are tied only to a random ID, not to you; to ask about them, write to us and include the support ID shown in the app if you can.

Children

Mangio is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address to deliver the page. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. If a change matters for how your data is used, the app will tell you.
