Questions

Good to know

Is Mangio free?

Yes. The diary, the built-in foods, barcode scanning, recipes, and backup and restore are free. Premium adds deeper analysis and removes the ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Mangio never asks for your name, email or phone number, and every feature works without signing up.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. Your foods, meals, water, weight and goals are stored in the app, and every number you see is worked out on the phone. What you log is never sent to a server, to statistics, to crash reports or to ads.

The privacy policy lists exactly what the app does send, such as a barcode when you look one up.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Make a backup from time to time: Mangio saves all of your data to a file, and you choose where it goes. Restore it on your new phone. Android's own backup to your Google account, if it is on, can also bring your data across.

Because there is no account and no server, nobody else has a copy, so keeping a backup is up to you.

Does Mangio show ads?

The free app shows light ads from Google. Nothing you log is ever passed to them: no food, meal, water, weight or goal. Where the law asks, you choose whether ads can be personalised. Premium removes the ads.

Does Mangio tell me what to eat?

No. Mangio does not follow a diet or judge what you eat. Goals are worked out from general formulas and from what you enter, and you can change them or ignore them. Nothing in the app is medical advice; talk to a doctor or a dietitian before you change how you eat.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with Android 12 or later.
