Foods you actually eat

1,235 foods, curated one by one.

The built-in foods are not a copy of a national table. Each one was chosen for the meals people eat in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Austria, France, Italy and Brazil, taken from the best public nutrient table for it, then checked and completed one food at a time. They are on your phone, so search works offline.

  • Servings the way people eat them. One apple, a spoon of oil, a glass of milk in Italy, a cup of rice in the United States. Each serving is weighed from USDA's household measures, a country's own book of portions or its national guidelines.
  • Gaps filled, never guessed. When a table did not measure a nutrient, another table that measured the same food fills it in. A value nobody measured is never written as zero, and the app tells you when a food's data is partial.
  • Honest about precision. An apple is measured. A croissant is a typical recipe. A pizza is an estimate, because it changes from one kitchen to the next. Each food says which.
  • Found by the words you use. Everyday and regional names find the food, and typos are corrected.

Questions

Good to know

Is Mangio free?

Yes. The diary, the built-in foods, barcode scanning, recipes, and backup and restore are free. Premium adds deeper analysis and removes the ads.

Where do the nutrition values come from?

The 1,235 built-in foods come from public national tables: USDA FoodData Central, CoFID, Ciqual, BLS, TACO, and the Swiss and Swedish databases. Each food was checked one by one, given servings the way people eat it in each country, and completed from another table where one measured what the first did not.

Packaged foods come from Open Food Facts when you scan a barcode, and you can add your own foods.

Do I need an account?

No. Mangio never asks for your name, email or phone number, and every feature works without signing up.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. Your foods, meals, water, weight and goals are stored in the app, and every number you see is worked out on the phone. What you log is never sent to a server, to statistics, to crash reports or to ads.

The privacy policy lists exactly what the app does send, such as a barcode when you look one up.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Make a backup from time to time: Mangio saves all of your data to a file, and you choose where it goes. Restore it on your new phone. Android's own backup to your Google account, if it is on, can also bring your data across.

Because there is no account and no server, nobody else has a copy, so keeping a backup is up to you.

Does Mangio show ads?

The free app shows light ads from Google. Nothing you log is ever passed to them: no food, meal, water, weight or goal. Where the law asks, you choose whether ads can be personalised. Premium removes the ads.

Does Mangio tell me what to eat?

No. Mangio does not follow a diet or judge what you eat. Goals are worked out from general formulas and from what you enter, and you can change them or ignore them. Nothing in the app is medical advice; talk to a doctor or a dietitian before you change how you eat.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with Android 12 or later.
